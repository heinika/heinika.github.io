Dataset: China City Attraction Details
Author: Audrey Hengrui Zhang
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/audreyhengruizhang/china-city-attraction-details
License: Apache 2.0

The dataset documentation states that the attraction metadata was collected
from https://travel.qunar.com/. This project stores a reduced local index and
loads referenced images on demand rather than redistributing the image files.
